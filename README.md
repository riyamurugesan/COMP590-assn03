The user input I added:
- Pressing the Down Arrow key decreases the speed of the stickman's swinging.
-  Pressing the Up Arrow key increases the speed of the stickman's swinging.
   - Use it to increase the difficulty of the mechanic listed below!
- If you press the Space Bar right as the stickman reaches the top of his swing (when he's moving forwards), he will smile BIG!
    - If the space bar is clicked and he is **_not_** at the top of his swing, he will frown :(
